====================================================================================================
1. THE ORACLE GATE -- intention-to-treat over every scored instance in the arm
====================================================================================================
Unit: the instance. Population: every instance the arm scored (never-revised
instances contribute a delta of exactly 0, they are not dropped).
keep-best  = reject every transition with post < pre, keep the best text seen.
stop-first = halt the loop at the first transition that would lower F1.
report-only= never revise; ship the round-1 draft.

(CLEAR's per-item accuracy). recall alone = attribution; precision alone =
whether what the draft said about that item was correct.
